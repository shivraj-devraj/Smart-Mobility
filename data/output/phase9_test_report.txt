12. Known Limitations
---------------------
Status: WARNING

- The project is historical and batch-based, not real-time.
- No hourly observations are available.
- Direction, GPS coordinates, FASTag transactions, and traffic-signal event streams are unavailable or not integrated.
- The Decision Tree evaluation is based on the chronological test period 2024-02-01 to 2024-08-09 and should not be described as a guarantee of future performance.
- Native Spark PipelineModel persistence is unavailable in this Windows environment because Hadoop Windows JNI persistence fails. The project retains a reproducible model specification instead.
- Festival and holiday fields use the project's curated Karnataka calendar; it is not presented as an official government dataset.
- Advisory outputs are deterministic recommendations and do not claim physical traffic-control automation or deployment.

Phase 10 was not started.
